Key takeaway

Daily weather records show what happened during the season. Climate normals provide the reference needed to judge whether that weather was typical or unusual. Gardeners use both kinds of information.

Data sources: Applied Climate Information System (ACIS) daily station observations and NOAA 1991–2020 U.S. Climate Normals for Corvallis State University.
