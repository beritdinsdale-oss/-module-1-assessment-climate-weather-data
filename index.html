💡 Garden Detective Tip

To find the average temperature departure for several months, add the values in the Difference column and divide by the number of months.

June: +0.9°F July: +5.2°F August: −0.5°F (+0.9 + 5.2 − 0.5) ÷ 3 = +1.9°F

Averages summarize the overall pattern. Monthly and daily values show how conditions changed within that period.

Key takeaway

Averages describe the overall picture, but they do not show how conditions were distributed through time. Gardeners need both climate summaries and daily weather information to understand what plants experienced.
